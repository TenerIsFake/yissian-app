Dialect Rules YISSIAN

Every word keeps its onset (initial consonant cluster). The vowel nucleus determines which suffix replaces the rest.

-issFront / short vowels

Words with a, e, i, ay, ea, ai nuclei become onset + -iss

cat → ciss snake → sniss hell → hiss yeah → yiss
-rissBack / resonant vowels

Words with o, ou, ow, oo, ue nuclei and magic-e (a/i/o+Ce) become onset + -riss

cool → criss right → riss blade → bladriss great → griss
-ridCompletive endings

Suffixes -er, -le, -ness, -ment, -ful; r-colored vowels; consonant+y triggers completive form

better → bettrid beautiful → beautifrid arm → armrid fries → frid
-issin'Gerundive (-ing)

Words ending in -ing or -in' become onset + -issin'

running → rissin' going → gissin' fucking → fissin'
VerbatimPreserved unchanged

Function words (a, the, and, I, is…), numbers, URLs, and punctuation are never transformed.

the → the 42 → 42 and → and
